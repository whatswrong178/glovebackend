The Collection

Three series.
One standard.

Medical grade. Nothing less. Each series is engineered for a specific discipline — but all share a single uncompromising threshold.

Series 01

Nitrile

Powder-Free · Latex-Free · Medical Grade

Superior barrier strength without allergenic risk. Textured fingertips for reliable grip. Available in standard and extended cuff lengths. Certified to FDA 510(K), EN455, and ASTM D6319. For those who demand precision — from operating theatres to tattoo studios.

Series 02

Latex

Natural Rubber · Powder-Free · Medical Grade

Natural rubber latex — the original choice of surgeons. Unmatched elasticity and form-fit conformity. Light but strong. The tactile benchmark against which all other gloves are measured. For clinicians who trust their hands above everything.

Series 03

Antimicrobial
Nitrile

AMG™ · Non-Leaching · Medical Grade

The world's first non-leaching antimicrobial examination glove. A photosensitiser on the outer surface generates singlet oxygen upon light exposure — killing up to 99.999% of microbes on contact, including Human Coronavirus. The glove itself is a line of defence.
