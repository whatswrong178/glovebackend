你面临的挑战 The SME Challenge

中小企业，长期在吃亏 SMEs Have Always Paid More

采购量小，话语权弱——这不是你的错，是游戏规则本来就不公平。 Small orders, weak bargaining power — the game was never designed to be fair for SMEs.

💸

以零售价格支付商业采购Paying Retail for Business Quantities

大公司一次采购数千件，享受超低折扣。你采购几十件，却要付接近零售的价格——同一个产品，不同的待遇。 Large companies order thousands and get massive discounts. You order dozens and pay near-retail — same product, completely different treatment.

🙈

供应商只看大客户Suppliers Only Care About Large Buyers

供货不稳定、响应慢、服务态度差——当你的订单对供应商来说"可有可无"时，这就是你得到的对待。 Unreliable supply, slow responses, poor service — when your orders are optional to suppliers, this is what you get.

📦

最低订购量压垮现金流Minimum Orders Crush Your Cash Flow

你只需要100个，但最低起订是500个。资金被占用，库存积压，本来可以用来发展业务的钱全部锁死。 You need 100 units but minimum order is 500. Capital locked up, inventory piling — money that could grow your business, frozen.

打破规模歧视 Collective Purchasing
Breaks the Scale Bias
